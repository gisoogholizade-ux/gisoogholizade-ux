UMASIL

A multi-role marketplace project covering customer, seller and admin workflows, authentication, orders, transactions, contracts, dashboards and deployment.

Product challenge

Build a business platform where different user roles can complete distinct workflows while sharing a reliable data and transaction layer.

What we implemented

  • Buyer, seller and admin experiences
  • Authentication and role-based access
  • Order and transaction workflows
  • Contract and operational flows
  • Database-driven dashboards

Engineering coverage

Backend logic, REST-style integrations, SQL/database work, server-side workflows, frontend implementation, testing and production deployment.

Why it matters

This project demonstrates end-to-end delivery on a real product rather than a static portfolio concept.
