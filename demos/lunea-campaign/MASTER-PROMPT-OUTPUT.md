**Secondary Brand Color:** #F3ECE8

**Extra Notes:** The site is a fictional portfolio concept created to demonstrate campaign landing-page design, UI/UX, CRO, responsive implementation, and front-end development.

---

## Step 1 — Design Concept

The visual direction combines premium editorial beauty branding with a conversion-first e-commerce layout. Instead of using a generic WooCommerce-style grid, the page uses large whitespace, oversized Persian typography, soft neutral surfaces, rounded cards, restrained shadows, muted rose and sage accents, and custom CSS product mockups.

The hero is intentionally emotional and product-led, while the rest of the page gradually reduces uncertainty through pricing clarity, social proof, a simple routine explanation, guarantees, FAQs, and repeated but natural CTAs.

---

## Step 2 — Why This Structure

The page follows a CRO-driven sequence:

1. **Campaign announcement:** instantly communicates urgency and offer.
2. **Hero:** establishes the brand promise and primary CTA.
3. **Trust metrics:** adds confidence before asking the user to buy.
4. **Countdown:** creates urgency without interrupting the experience.
5. **Campaign products:** makes the offer tangible and easy to compare.
6. **3-step routine:** reduces choice overload and explains product usage.
7. **Benefits:** answers practical objections about product and delivery.
8. **Verified-style testimonials:** adds social proof near the purchase decision.
9. **FAQ:** removes remaining friction before conversion.
10. **Final CTA + mobile sticky CTA:** captures users who are ready to act at different points in the journey.

Only sections that support the campaign goal are included; unnecessary portfolio, about, services, pricing-table, or contact sections were intentionally omitted.

## Step 3 — Website Content

All visible Persian copy inside `index.html` was written specifically for the LUNÉA concept. No Lorem Ipsum or generic placeholder copy is used.

Key campaign message:

> روتین پوستی که واقعاً دلت می‌خواهد ادامه‌اش بدهی.

Primary CTA:

> خرید با تخفیف کمپین

Supporting conversion themes include limited-time discounting, free shipping threshold, verified-style customer reviews, simplified product selection, a 3-step routine, and a 7-day return policy for unopened products.

---

## Step 4 — Production-Ready Front-End

The implementation is split into:

- `index.html` — semantic RTL page structure and real Persian content
- `styles.css` — responsive visual system, custom product art, layouts, hover states, mobile CTA and animations
- `script.js` — countdown timer, FAQ accordion, add-to-cart feedback, and viewport reveal interactions

The demo is designed to work on desktop, tablet, and mobile without Bootstrap or a generic UI framework.
